## 第 3 輪｜PHP-FPM 進程管理器 static、ondemand 與 dynamic 行為定義
- 時間：2026-11-22 01:53
- 網址：https://www.php.net/manual/en/install.fpm.configuration.php
- 逐字摘句：static - the number of child processes is fixed ( pm.max_children ). ondemand - the processes spawn on demand (when requested, as opposed to dynamic, where pm.start_servers are started when the service is started). The number of child processes is limited by pm.max_children , and idle processes are killed after pm.process_idle_timeout . dynamic - the number of child processes is set dynamically based on the following directives: pm.max_children , pm.start_servers , pm.min_spare_servers , pm.max_spare_servers .
- 核心事實：PHP-FPM 提供 static（固定子進程數）、ondemand（有請求時才生成進程且閒置被砍）與 dynamic（依負載動態調整子進程數）三種模式。

- 時間：2026-11-22 02:00
- 網址：https://www.sqlite.org/wal.html
- 逐字摘句：WAL provides more concurrency as readers do not block writers and a writer does not block readers. Reading and writing can proceed concurrently.
