17. **第 01 輪｜FActScore（原子事實分解與引用驗證）**：
   - **核心事實**：FACTSCORE 提出將文本拆解為一系列原子事實（atomic facts），並以可靠知識源所支持之原子事實比例來客觀驗收事實度。
   - **出處**：https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.14251
   - **證據網址**：https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.14251
   - **原文摘句**：In this paper, we introduce FACTSCORE, a new evaluation that breaks a generation into a series of atomic facts and computes the percentage of atomic facts supported by a reliable knowledge source.

18. **第 02 輪｜SelfCheckGPT（零知識庫隨機採樣一致性檢測）**：
   - **核心事實**：SelfCheckGPT 透過對同一任務進行多次隨機採樣，以多次回報之間的事實一致性來檢驗代理人是否存在幻覺與自相矛盾。
   - **出處**：https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.08896
   - **證據網址**：https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.08896
   - **原文摘句**：SelfCheckGPT leverages the simple idea that if an LLM has knowledge of a given concept, sampled responses are likely to be similar and contain consistent facts. However, for hallucinated facts, stochastically sampled responses are likely to diverge and contradict one another.

19. **第 03 輪｜SWE-bench（代碼代理人客觀物理單元測試驗收）**：
   - **核心事實**：SWE-bench 透過真實代碼倉庫與執行環境交互，要求模型跨函數與檔案協同修改，以單元測試結果嚴格驗收代理人解決實際工程問題之能力。
   - **出處**：https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.06770
   - **證據網址**：https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.06770
   - **原文摘句**：Resolving issues in SWE-bench frequently requires understanding and coordinating changes across multiple functions, classes, and even files simultaneously, calling for models to interact with execution environments, process extremely long contexts and perform complex reasoning that goes far beyond traditional code generation tasks.

20. **第 04 輪｜ALCE（大模型自動引用評估基準）**：
   - **核心事實**：ALCE 提出大語言模型自動引用評估基準，要求構建端到端檢索支持證據之系統，用以嚴格驗收代理人生成的回答與出處引用品質。
   - **出處**：https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.14627
   - **證據網址**：https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.14627
   - **原文摘句**：We propose ALCE, the first benchmark for Automatic LLMs' Citation Evaluation. ALCE collects a diverse set of questions and retrieval corpora and requires building end-to-end systems to retrieve supporting evidence and generate answers with citations.

21. **第 05 輪｜Process Supervision（過程級逐步驗證優於結果監督）**：
   - **核心事實**：研究實證過程監督相較於結果監督能顯著提升推理能力，其過程監督模型在 MATH 測試集子集達到 78% 的解題成功率。
   - **出處**：https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.20050
   - **證據網址**：https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.20050
   - **原文摘句**：We conduct our own investigation, finding that process supervision significantly outperforms outcome supervision for training models to solve problems from the challenging MATH dataset. Our process-supervised model solves 78% of problems from a representative subset of the MATH test set.

22. **第 06 輪｜Multi-Agent Debate（多代理人對抗辯論降低幻覺）**：
   - **核心事實**：多代理人辯論框架透過多個模型實例提出論點並相互質疑辯論，能在多輪交互中達成共識並顯著降低單一代理人的謬誤與幻覺。
   - **出處**：https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.14325
   - **證據網址**：https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.14325
   - **原文摘句**：In this paper, we present a complementary approach to improve language responses where multiple language model instances propose and debate their individual responses and reasoning processes over multiple rounds to arrive at a common final answer.

23. **第 07 輪｜FactCC（基於弱監督模型的事實一致性驗證）**：
   - **核心事實**：FactCC 提出基於弱監督模型的事實一致性驗證方法，專門識別代理人生成的回報內容與原始來源文檔之間的事實衝突。
   - **出處**：https://arxiv.org/abs/1910.12840
   - **證據網址**：https://arxiv.org/abs/1910.12840
   - **原文摘句**：We propose a weakly-supervised, model-based approach for verifying factual consistency and identifying conflicts between source documents and a generated summary.

24. **第 08 輪｜HaluEval（大規模幻覺評測基準與自我識別）**：
   - **核心事實**：HaluEval 構建包含生成與人工標註樣本之大規模基準，深入評估語言模型在不同情境中識別幻覺之能力。
   - **出處**：https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.11747
   - **證據網址**：https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.11747
   - **原文摘句**：To understand what types of content and to which extent LLMs are apt to hallucinate, we introduce the Hallucination Evaluation benchmark for Large Language Models (HaluEval), a large collection of generated and human-annotated hallucinated samples for evaluating the performance of LLMs in recognizing hallucination.

25. **第 09 輪｜Ragas（無參考檢索增強生成評估框架）**：
   - **核心事實**：Ragas 提出無須人工標註真值的評估框架，從檢索數據庫與語言模型生成模組等多維度自動驗收代理人的生成品質並降低幻覺風險。
   - **出處**：https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.15217
   - **證據網址**：https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.15217
   - **原文摘句**：We introduce Ragas (Retrieval Augmented Generation Assessment), a framework for reference-free evaluation of Retrieval Augmented Generation (RAG) pipelines. RAG systems are composed of a retrieval and an LLM based generation module, and provide LLMs with knowledge from a reference textual database, which enables them to act as a natural language layer between a user and textual databases, reducing the risk of hallucinations.

26. **第 10 輪｜AgentBench（多環境大模型代理人綜合基準）**：
   - **核心事實**：AgentBench 提出包含 8 個不同環境的多維度基準，用以評估大語言模型作為代理人之推理與決策執行能力。
   - **出處**：https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.03688
   - **證據網址**：https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.03688
   - **原文摘句**：We present AgentBench, a multi-dimensional benchmark that consists of 8 distinct environments to assess LLM-as-Agent's reasoning and decision-making abilities.
