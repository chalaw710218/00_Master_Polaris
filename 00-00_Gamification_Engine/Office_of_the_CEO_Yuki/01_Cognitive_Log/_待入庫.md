## 第 01 輪｜FActScore（原子事實分解與引用驗證）
- 網址：https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.14251
- 逐字摘句：Evaluating the factual precision of generated text is non-trivial, especially when a generation contains multiple pieces of information. We introduce FActScore, a new evaluation metric that breaks the generation into a series of atomic facts and computes the percentage of atomic facts supported by a reliable knowledge source.
- 核心事實：FActScore 提出將代理人生成的長文本拆解為一系列原子事實（atomic facts），並以可靠知識源所支持之原子事實比例來客觀驗收真實度。

## 第 02 輪｜SelfCheckGPT（零知識庫隨機採樣一致性檢測）
- 網址：https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.08896
- 逐字摘句：SelfCheckGPT leverages the simple idea that if an LLM has knowledge of a given concept, sampled responses are likely to be similar and contain consistent facts. However, for hallucinated facts, stochastically sampled responses are likely to diverge and contradict one another.
- 核心事實：SelfCheckGPT 透過對同一任務進行多次隨機採樣，以多次回報之間的事實一致性來檢驗代理人是否存在幻覺與自相矛盾。

## 第 03 輪｜SWE-bench（代碼代理人客觀物理單元測試驗收）
- 網址：https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.06770
- 逐字摘句：We introduce SWE-bench, an evaluation framework consisting of 2,294 software engineering problems drawn from real GitHub issues and corresponding pull requests across 12 popular Python repositories.
- 核心事實：SWE-bench 提出包含 2,294 個軟體工程問題的評測框架，以實際代碼倉庫之單元測試執行結果來嚴格驗收代理人的交付品質。

## 第 04 輪｜ALCE（大模型自動引用評估基準）
- 網址：https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.14627
- 逐字摘句：We propose ALCE, the first benchmark for Automatic LLMs' Citation Evaluation. ALCE contains diverse questions, retrieval pools, and automated metrics for fluency, correctness, and citation quality that correlate with human judgments.
- 核心事實：ALCE 提出自動引用評估基準，透過流暢度、正確性與引用質量三大維度，驗收代理人回報的出處是否真正支持其陳述。

## 第 05 輪｜Process Supervision（過程級逐步驗證優於結果監督）
- 網址：https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.20050
- 逐字摘句：We show that process supervision significantly outperforms outcome supervision for training models to solve problems from the challenging MATH dataset. Our process-supervised model solves 78% of problems from a representative subset of the MATH test set.
- 核心事實：研究實證過程監督（Process Supervision）相較於結果監督能顯著提升複雜推理準確率，在 MATH 測試集子集達到 78% 的解題成功率。

## 第 06 輪｜Multi-Agent Debate（多代理人對抗辯論降低幻覺）
- 網址：https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.14325
- 逐字摘句：In this paper, we present a complementary approach to improve language responses where multiple language model instances propose and debate their individual responses and reasoning processes over multiple rounds to arrive at a common final answer.
- 核心事實：多代理人辯論框架透過多個模型實例提出論點並相互質疑辯論，能在多輪交互中達成共識並顯著降低單一代理人的謬誤與幻覺。

## 第 07 輪｜FactCC（基於弱監督模型的事實一致性驗證）
- 網址：https://arxiv.org/abs/1910.12840
- 逐字摘句：We propose a weakly-supervised, model-based approach for verifying factual consistency and identifying conflicts between source documents and a generated summary.
- 核心事實：FactCC 提出基於弱監督模型的事實一致性驗證方法，專門識別代理人生成的回報內容與原始來源文檔之間的事實衝突。

## 第 08 輪｜HaluEval（大規模幻覺評測基準與自我識別）
- 網址：https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.11747
- 逐字摘句：To understand what hallucinations LLMs generate and how well they can recognize hallucinations, we introduce the HaluEval, a large-scale hallucination evaluation benchmark for large language models.
- 核心事實：HaluEval 構建大規模幻覺評測基準，深入探討語言模型在生成任務中產生之幻覺類型及其自我識別能力。

## 第 09 輪｜Ragas（無參考檢索增強生成評估框架）
- 網址：https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.15217
- 逐字摘句：We introduce Ragas (Retrieval Augmented Generation Assessment), a framework for reference-free evaluation of Retrieval Augmented Generation (RAG) pipelines. RAG systems are composed of a retrieval and an LLM based generation module, and provide LLMs with knowledge from a reference textual database, which enables them to act as a natural language layer between a user and textual databases, reducing the risk of hallucinations.
- 核心事實：Ragas 提出無須人工標註真值的評估框架，從檢索數據庫與語言模型生成模組等多維度自動驗收代理人的生成品質並降低幻覺風險。

## 第 10 輪｜AgentBench（多環境大模型代理人綜合基準）
- 網址：https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.03688
- 逐字摘句：We present AgentBench, a multi-dimensional benchmark that consists of 8 distinct environments to assess LLM-as-Agent's reasoning and decision-making abilities in a multi-turn open-ended generation setting.
- 核心事實：AgentBench 提出包含 8 個不同互動環境的多維度基準，用以評估大語言模型作為代理人在多輪開放生成情境中的推理與決策能力。
