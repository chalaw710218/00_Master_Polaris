摘句：We conduct our own investigation, finding that process supervision significantly outperforms outcome supervision for training models to solve problems from the challenging MATH dataset. Our process-supervised model solves 78% of problems from a representative subset of the MATH test set.
核心事實：過程監督模型在 MATH 測試集子集解決了 78% 的問題。
