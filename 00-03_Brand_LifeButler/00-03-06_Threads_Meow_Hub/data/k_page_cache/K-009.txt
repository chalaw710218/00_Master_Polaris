                        Member in Focus: Oldenburg, Germany 	 
		 
                      
              The city of Oldenburg has pursued a systematic strategy to promote age-friendliness since 2012, guided by the eight domains of the WHO “Age-Friendly Cities” framework. With the unanimously adopted 2025 agenda “Self-determined and Right in the Middle!”, the City Council approved a comprehensive action plan that integrates four key areas: social participation, health and care, housing and mobility, as well as governance and coordination. 
 In the field of information, counselling and social participation, the Senior and Care Support Centre (SPN) provides low-threshold guidance on care, housing, social support, and volunteering opportunities. This is complemented by decentralised older adult support projects across all districts, fostering social inclusion directly within neighbourhoods.      Age-friendly Practice in Focus: URBANAGE – Shaping More Age-Friendly Cities Through Digital Innovation 
	 
		 
                      
              In a context of demographic change and urbanisation, the European Horizon 2020 project “URBANAGE” is aiming at creating a digital ecosystem to foster inclusive short- and long-term decision-making in the field of urban planning. The platform URBANAGE will make it easier for urban planners, decision and policy makers, and older adults to better co-design and develop inclusive age-friendly cities, where people can age and enjoy their living environment, as they are wishing to.

          Age-friendly Environments ECHO Learning Programme (AFE-ECHO) 
 
	     ©️ WHO / A. Tardy  
 Are you a new or emerging leader of age-friendly cities and communities? Do you want to join a community of age-friendly champions and develop the skills required to create better places to grow, live, work, play, and age? 
 Participate in the Age-friendly Environments ECHO Programme (AFE-ECHO): a multi-session learning cycle part of the MENTOR-AFE programme aligned with the latest approaches and guidance on age-friendly environments from WHO. Participants will be taken through the main components of the WHO age-friendly framework by global experts. Each session will also create a space for participants to apply what they learn through collaborative problem-solving.

     The Government of the State of Paraná – our newest sub-national Affiliate 	 
		 
                      
              O Estado do Paraná está localizado na região sul do Brasil, no qual vivem mais de 11milhões de habitantes, distribuídos em 399 municípios. Com uma economia dinâmica e diversificada, o Paraná é a quarta maior economia do Brasil. Assim como as regiões mais desenvolvidas do mundo, o Paraná se depara com o rápido envelhecimento populacional, mais expressivo nos municípios menores. 
 Em 2023 foi criado, no Paraná, a Secretaria de Estado da Mulher, Igualdade Racial e Pessoa Idosa, que é a pasta responsável, hoje, pela política de garantia de direitos da pessoa idosa.
