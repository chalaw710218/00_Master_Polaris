1. **[K-002] 遞歸模型崩潰與數據分佈長尾消失**：
  - **核心事實**：以模型生成的內容進行訓練會對產生的模型造成不可逆的缺陷，導致原始內容分佈的長尾消失（稱為模型崩潰，發生於 VAE、GMM 與 LLM）。
  - **出處**：https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.17493
  - **證據網址**：https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.17493
  - **原文摘句**：We find that use of model-generated content in training causes irreversible defects in the resulting models, where tails of the original content distribution disappear. We refer to this effect as Model Collapse and show that it can occur in Variational Autoencoders, Gaussian Mixture Models and LLMs.

2. **[K-005] 第三空間定義與社區營造**：
  - **核心事實**：第三空間是社會學家 Ray Oldenburg 創造的詞彙，指人們在家（第一空間）與工作（第二空間）之間度過時間的場所。
  - **出處**：https://www.brookings.edu/articles/third-places-as-community-builders/
  - **證據網址**：https://www.brookings.edu/articles/third-places-as-community-builders/
  - **原文摘句**：Third places is a term coined by sociologist Ray Oldenburg and refers to places where people spend time between home ('first' place) and work ('second' place).

4. **[K-008] 演算法放大早期高速互動**：
  - **核心事實**：社群媒體演算法作為主要分發過濾器，會大幅放大捕捉到早期高速互動（如收藏與分享）的內容，並主動將被動低努力的內容埋沒於競爭信號之下。
  - **出處**：https://sproutsocial.com/insights/social-media-algorithms/
  - **證據網址**：https://sproutsocial.com/insights/social-media-algorithms/
  - **原文摘句**：Social media algorithms act as primary distribution filters. They heavily amplify content that captures early, high-velocity engagement like saves and shares, while actively burying passive, low-effort content beneath more competitive signals.
